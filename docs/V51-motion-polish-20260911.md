# v5.1 motion polish — implementation record

Baseline: `d6b1e5f`, preserved by local tag `v5-before-motion-polish-20260911`.

## Accepted behavior

- Free arms use connected shoulder/elbow/wrist articulation and hanging sleeves through pickup, dragging, falling and landing.
- Climbing completes one exchange cycle in 1.0 seconds, preserving per-cycle rise and matching planted contact to window movement. Knee/leg volume and gathered skirt silhouette are revised without global limb stretching.
- Swing motion has one continuous phase across active/idle/top-cleaning states. Existing 1.02/3.87 second periods transition smoothly over 1.2 seconds. Pause freezes phase; resume excludes paused elapsed time.
- Swing petting is a 3-second facial/head overlay with both hands on the ropes, following the existing gesture and 6-second cooldown. Its hit region follows the actual head.
- Four cleaning poses use a small brush, an explicit take/sweep/stow sequence, readable grip and dust emitted at the brush tip. The planted hand remains attached on a wall or rope.
- Local paint changes may improve folds, cuffs, grip and leg volume while retaining character identity and costume colors/patterns.

## Interface boundaries

New brush/arm model parameters are declared in the exact native inventory. Existing operation IDs, cleaner authorization and result classification are unchanged.

The first packaging regression stopped after 45 climbing tests passed and the free-arm group reported 27 passes and two failures. It produced no package. The other failure required every sleeve mesh triangle to retain more than 95% of its area, an assumption from the former nearly rigid sleeve. The approved hanging garment deliberately gathers cloth. The final saved IR was evaluated at 31 phases: all nondegenerate triangles retained orientation; conservative painted-region minimum area ratios were 0.3703/0.3895 for sleeves and 0.4794/0.7686 for hands, with nonzero local singular values and preserved overall painted area.
